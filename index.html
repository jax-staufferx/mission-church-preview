What a Sunday is like

Plan your visit

  1. 8:30a.m.

    Breakfast

    Please come enjoy a continental breakfast with us from 8:30 to 9:20: coffee, pastries, breakfast sandwiches, and conversation with friends.

  2. 9:30a.m.

    Worship

    A blend of contemporary Christian music and the old hymns set to keyboards, drums and guitars. Please join us in worshiping Him!

  3. Every Sunday

    Sunday School

    The children are divided by grades into classes, from toddler through 8th grade.

Need help?

Because of our inner-city location, neighbors often come to us with immediate needs.

  • Food

    We give out perishable food items, including chicken and milk, on Sunday mornings after our 9:30 a.m. service.

  • Laundry

    Our Laundry Room Ministry makes it possible to wash and dry a load of laundry on Sunday mornings during our church service. We have five washing machines and five dryers, so come at 8:30 to help ensure that a machine will be available for you.

  • Bike repairs

    Heart of the City Bicycles offers free repairs at our building to anyone who can’t afford them. The bike shop is open on Friday mornings, between 9:00 a.m. and noon. Use door 3 off the north parking lot.

Have a question? You may find the answer in our FAQ.

Welcome!
