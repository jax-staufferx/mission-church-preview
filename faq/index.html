Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most.

What time is church on Sunday?

Coffee and pastries are served at 8:30 a.m., and worship starts at 9:30 a.m. every Sunday at 1651 Cass Street in Fort Wayne.

Plan your visit
Is there something for my kids?

Yes. We have Sunday School classes for children, from toddler through 8th grade.

Children's Ministry
What should I wear?

Wear whatever you're comfortable in.

How can I get free food?

We give out perishable food items, including chicken and milk, on Sunday mornings after our 9:30 a.m. service.

Food Ministry
How can I use the Laundry Room?

Our Laundry Room Ministry makes it possible to wash and dry a load of laundry on Sunday mornings during our church service. We have five washing machines and five dryers, so come at 8:30 to help ensure that a machine will be available for you.

Laundry Room
When can I get my bike repaired?

Heart of the City Bicycles offers free repairs at our building to anyone who can’t afford them. The bike shop is open on Friday mornings, between 9:00 a.m. and noon. Use door 3 off the north parking lot.

Bicycle Ministry
How can I give?

You can give by card online or by Venmo to @MissionChurchFW.

Give
How do I reach someone at the church?

Our phone line, (260) 222-7135, is voicemail only, so please leave a message. You can also email MissionChurchFW@gmail.com or use the form on our Visit page.

Send a message

Have a question? Please leave a message at (260) 222-7135.
